## Coolbet project

Coolbet project, with the goal to establish data integrity, define key performance indicators, and extract actionable business insights. 

- Transform a month of raw gaming data into a powerful narrative for the leadership. The deliverable should be a dynamic, insight-led dashboard or Report Interface.
- Prove the integrity of the information by cleaning both obvious inconsistencies and the deepest structural flaws that could secretly skew the results.
- Once the data is robust, clearly define and present the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) that matter most.
- Finally, use your analysis to dive deep, uncover the hidden anomalies and sudden shifts, and structure the entire report as a compelling story that leads to business insights.

The data visualization is available here: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/andrea.lucini.paioni7588/viz/Coolbetdashboard/GeneralOverview?publish=yes
